Character reference edge cases

Every form below has to be decoded exactly once. Decoding twice turns the double-escaped case into a bare ampersand and silently corrupts the text; decoding zero times leaves raw entity references in output that claims to be plain Markdown. Both failures are easy to introduce and hard to notice without a fragment that carries all the forms side by side.

Named: & < > " © — …

Numeric decimal: € — ©

Numeric hexadecimal: € — ©

Non-breaking space between these words.

An ampersand in ordinary prose: R & D, and the expression 5 && 6.

Double-escaped: &amp; has to survive as the literal text &amp;.
